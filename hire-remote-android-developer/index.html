Both. A fixed price suits a clear scope; a day rate suits ongoing work on an existing app or a project that will change as you learn from users.

Which time zones do you cover?

I work on Beirut time, UTC+2 in winter and UTC+3 in summer. That overlaps the full working day in Europe and the Gulf, and the morning on the US East Coast.

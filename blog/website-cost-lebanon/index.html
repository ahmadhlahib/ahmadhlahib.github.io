How much does a website cost in Lebanon? 2026 prices in USD

Quotes for the same website in Beirut can range from $150 to $5,000. Here is what you actually get at each price, a worked example, and the yearly costs to plan for.

By Ahmad El Lahib · freelance developer in Baabda, Lebanon

Short answer: with a freelance web developer in Lebanon in 2026, a one-page landing page costs about $200–500, a company website in Arabic and English $400–1,500, a catalogue or ordering site $1,500–3,500, and a web app with logins and a dashboard $3,000–10,000 or more. Running it costs from about $10–20 a year for the domain, plus hosting and small updates.

What moves the price

  1. Number of pages and how ready the content is. Ten pages with texts and photos ready is faster than five pages where the developer has to chase every paragraph.
  2. Arabic and English. A bilingual site is close to two sites. Arabic needs a real right-to-left layout and an Arabic font that reads well on a phone.
  3. Custom design or a ready theme. A theme adjusted to your brand is cheaper; a design made from scratch takes longer.
  4. Features. Forms, booking, a product catalogue you edit yourself, logins and dashboards each add real work.
  5. Google setup. Some cheap quotes skip it. Without titles, a sitemap, Search Console and a Business Profile, the site exists but nobody finds it.
  6. Speed on a phone. Most visitors in Lebanon are on a phone, often on mobile data. Making a site load fast takes care with images and code.

Yearly costs to plan for

  • Domain: a .com costs about $10–20 a year. Register it in your own name.
  • Hosting: a simple company site can be hosted for free or under $100 a year. Sites with logins and a database cost more as they grow.
  • Business email such as info@yourcompany.com, if you want it: free forwarding, or a few dollars per user per month for a full mailbox.
  • Updates: new photos, prices or a page now and then. Agree a small fixed price per update, or ask for a way to edit texts yourself.

Red flags in a website quote

  • The domain will be registered in the developer's name.
  • You don't get your own login to the hosting.
  • No mention of Google setup, Search Console or a sitemap.
  • No example sites you can open on your own phone.
  • "Arabic version" that is just a translation dropped into a left-to-right layout.
  • A very low price with a monthly fee you can never stop.

Four ways to spend less

  1. Have your texts, logo and photos ready before work starts.
  2. Start with fewer pages that answer your customers' real questions; add more later.
  3. Use a WhatsApp button instead of a complex booking system until you need one.
  4. Choose a static site with free or cheap hosting when you don't need logins.

FAQ

How much does a company website cost in Lebanon?

With a freelance developer in 2026, a company website of 5 to 8 pages in Arabic and English typically costs $400 to $1,500. A one-page landing page costs about $200 to $500.

How much does it cost to run a website every year?

A .com domain costs about $10 to $20 a year. Hosting for a simple company site can be free or under $100 a year; sites with logins and a database cost more. Add business email if you want it, and a small budget for updates.

Is Wix or WordPress cheaper than a custom website?

A builder like Wix is cheaper to start if you build it yourself, but you pay a monthly fee forever and the site stays on their platform. A custom site costs more once, is usually faster on phones and has very low running costs.

How long does it take to build a website in Lebanon?

A landing page takes a few days, a company website one to three weeks, and a web app with logins one to three months. Most delays come from waiting for texts and photos.

Will my new website show up on Google?

Only if it is set up for it: titles, descriptions, a sitemap, Search Console and a Google Business Profile with reviews. New sites usually take a few weeks to start ranking.

Who should own the domain and hosting?

You. The domain and hosting should be registered in your name or your company's name, with your own login, even if the developer manages them for you.

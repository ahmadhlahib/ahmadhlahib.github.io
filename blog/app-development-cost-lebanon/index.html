How much does an app cost in Lebanon? A 2026 guide

It's the first question every client asks, and the honest answer is "it depends". Here is exactly what it depends on, so you can estimate your own project before you talk to anyone.

By Ahmad El Lahib · freelance developer in Baabda, Lebanon

The six things that set the price

Every app quote, from a freelancer or an agency, is really an estimate of time. These are the things that add time:

  1. Platforms. Android only, iPhone only, or both. In Lebanon most users are on Android, so many businesses start there. When you need both, a shared codebase (Kotlin Multiplatform) costs far less than two separate apps.
  2. Number of screens and roles. A simple app has 5 to 10 screens for one kind of user. A delivery or booking app often has three apps in one: customer, driver and manager. Each role roughly multiplies the work.
  3. The backend. Does the app need accounts, a database, notifications or a dashboard for your staff? A ready-made backend such as Firebase keeps this small; connecting to an existing company system can take longer than the app itself.
  4. Offline use. Field and sales apps that must keep working without signal and sync later need careful design so nothing is lost. It's worth it, but it's real work.
  5. Arabic and English. A proper right-to-left Arabic layout is more than translated text. Plan for it from day one; adding it later costs more.
  6. Devices and integrations. Printers, barcode scanners, Bluetooth devices, maps and live tracking each add a piece of work and a round of testing on real hardware.

Typical timelines

  • Landing page or simple website: a few days.
  • Company website: one to three weeks, mostly waiting for content and photos.
  • First version of a focused app: about 4 to 10 weeks.
  • Multi-role app with a dashboard (like a taxi or delivery app): a few months, delivered in stages.

Multiply the time by a day rate and you have the order of magnitude of any honest quote.

The costs nobody mentions

  • Store accounts: Google Play charges a one-time registration fee; Apple's developer program is a yearly subscription. Open them in your company's name so you own the app.
  • Hosting and backend: Firebase and similar services are free or cheap at the start and grow with your users.
  • Domain and email for the website and the privacy policy the stores require.
  • Yearly upkeep: Google and Apple raise their requirements every year. An app that isn't updated can be hidden from the store. Budget a little each year for updates.

Fixed price or day rate?

A fixed price works when the scope is clear: you know the screens and features up front, and you pay for that result. A day rate suits projects that will change as you learn from users. Either way, insist on a written scope with milestones, and a working build you can try every week or two. That's how you stay in control of the budget.

Five ways to spend less

  1. Start with the smallest version that solves the main problem, then grow it with real feedback.
  2. Use one shared codebase for Android and iPhone.
  3. Use a managed backend instead of building your own servers.
  4. Have your texts, logo and photos ready before development starts.
  5. Work with a developer who uses AI coding tools and tests properly: faster delivery, fewer bugs to pay for later.

FAQ

Is it cheaper to hire a freelancer or an agency in Lebanon?

A freelancer usually costs less because there is no agency overhead, and you talk directly to the person writing the code. An agency makes sense when you need a large team at once.

Can I get an exact price before we talk?

Not honestly. A short call to understand the screens, users and devices is enough to send a written scope with a fixed price or a day rate.

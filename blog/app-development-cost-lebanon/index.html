How much does an app cost in Lebanon? 2026 prices in USD

It's the first question every client asks. Here are real price ranges, a worked example and exactly what moves the number, so you can estimate your own project before you talk to anyone.

By Ahmad El Lahib · freelance developer in Baabda, Lebanon

Short answer: with a freelance developer in Lebanon in 2026, a simple app costs about $2,000–5,000, a business app with a backend and a staff dashboard $5,000–15,000, and a multi-role app (customer, driver and manager) $15,000–35,000 or more. Agencies usually quote two to three times that. Add the store fees and about 10–20% of the build cost per year for upkeep.

The six things that set the price

Every app quote, from a freelancer or an agency, is really an estimate of time. These are the things that add time:

  1. Platforms. Android only, iPhone only, or both. In Lebanon most users are on Android, so many businesses start there. When you need both, a shared codebase (Kotlin Multiplatform) costs far less than two separate apps.
  2. Number of screens and roles. A simple app has 5 to 10 screens for one kind of user. A delivery or booking app often has three apps in one: customer, driver and manager. Each role roughly multiplies the work.
  3. The backend. Does the app need accounts, a database, notifications or a dashboard for your staff? A ready-made backend such as Firebase keeps this small; connecting to an existing company system can take longer than the app itself.
  4. Offline use. Field and sales apps that must keep working without signal and sync later need careful design so nothing is lost. With Lebanon's internet, it's often worth it, but it's real work.
  5. Arabic and English. A proper right-to-left Arabic layout is more than translated text. Plan for it from day one; adding it later costs more.
  6. Devices and integrations. Printers, barcode scanners, Bluetooth devices, maps and live tracking each add a piece of work and a round of testing on real hardware.

Whoever you pick, ask the 10 questions before you pay.

The costs nobody mentions

  • Store accounts: Google Play charges a one-time $25 fee; Apple's developer program is $99 per year. Open them in your company's name so you own the app.
  • Hosting and backend: Firebase and similar services are free at the start and grow with your users; most small apps pay little or nothing in the first year.
  • Domain and email for the website and the privacy policy the stores require, usually $15–40 a year.
  • Yearly upkeep: Google and Apple raise their requirements every year, and an app that isn't updated can be hidden from the store. Budget 10–20% of the build cost per year (more on this in keeping your app up to date).

Fixed price or day rate?

A fixed price works when the scope is clear: you know the screens and features up front, and you pay for that result. A day rate suits projects that will change as you learn from users. Either way, insist on a written scope with milestones, and a working build you can try every week or two. That's how you stay in control of the budget.

Five ways to spend less

  1. Start with the smallest version that solves the main problem, then grow it with real feedback.
  2. Start on Android, where most Lebanese users are, and use one shared codebase when you add iPhone.
  3. Use a managed backend instead of building your own servers.
  4. Have your texts, logo and photos ready before development starts.
  5. Work with a developer who uses AI coding tools and tests properly: faster delivery, fewer bugs to pay for later.

FAQ

How much does a simple app cost in Lebanon?

With a freelance developer in 2026, a simple Android app for one type of user with 5 to 10 screens usually costs about $2,000 to $5,000. Adding an iPhone version with a shared codebase adds roughly 30 to 50%.

How much does an Android app cost for a small business in Lebanon?

Most small-business Android apps land between $2,000 and $8,000: the low end for a catalogue, loyalty or booking app, the high end when it needs accounts, notifications and a dashboard for your staff.

Is it cheaper to hire a freelancer or an agency in Lebanon?

A freelancer usually costs less because there is no agency overhead, and you talk directly to the person writing the code. Agencies often quote two to three times more for the same app; they make sense when you need a large team at once.

How much does it cost to publish an app on Google Play and the App Store?

Google Play charges a one-time $25 registration fee. Apple's developer program costs $99 per year. Open both accounts in your company's name so you own the app.

How much does it cost to maintain an app every year?

Plan for about 10 to 20% of the build cost per year for Android and iOS updates, new store requirements, library security fixes and small changes, plus hosting that grows with your users.

How long does it take to build an app in Lebanon?

A focused first version takes about 4 to 8 weeks, a business app with a backend 2 to 4 months, and a multi-role app such as taxi or delivery 4 to 8 months delivered in stages.

Can I get an exact price before we talk?

Not honestly. A short call to understand the screens, users and devices is enough to send a written scope with a fixed price or a day rate.

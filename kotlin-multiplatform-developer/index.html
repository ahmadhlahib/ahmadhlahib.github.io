When KMP is the right choice

Good fit: business, booking, delivery and field apps where most of the work is data, rules and forms; teams that already know Kotlin; products that must launch on both stores with one small team.

Think twice: apps that are mostly heavy platform-specific UI or 3D, or a team that only knows Swift. I'll tell you honestly in the first call if native or another approach suits you better.

Questions clients ask

What is Kotlin Multiplatform?

Kotlin Multiplatform (KMP) lets Android and iOS apps share one Kotlin codebase for logic, data and, with Compose Multiplatform, the user interface, while still compiling to native apps on each platform.

How much cheaper is KMP than two native apps?

In my experience adding iOS to an Android app with a shared codebase adds roughly 30 to 50 percent, where a separate native iOS app costs close to double.

Is Kotlin Multiplatform production-ready?

Yes. Kotlin Multiplatform is stable and Compose Multiplatform for iOS is stable; both are used in production apps, including the one I shipped.

Can you add iOS to my existing Android app?

Usually yes, if it's written in Kotlin with a reasonably clean architecture. I start with a short code review and tell you what can be shared as it is and what needs refactoring first.

Do I need a Mac or an Apple developer account?

You need an Apple developer account in your company's name to publish. I handle the iOS builds and the App Store submission.

Do you work remotely with teams abroad?

Yes. I work remotely on Beirut time, UTC+2 or UTC+3, in English, Arabic and French, inside your repository and tools.

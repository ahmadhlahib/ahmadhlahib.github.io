About me

Payments taught me to build things that can't fail quietly.

As a Senior Android Developer at areeba, I develop the Android app that runs on our payment terminals — Nexgo and Kozen devices such as the UN20, N62 and N92. A customer taps a card, and my code reads it, talks to the bank, prints the receipt and tells the cash register or vending machine what happened.

That work means EMV card flows, PIN entry, reversals and currency conversion, plus integrations with cash registers over serial and Wi-Fi and with vending machines over MDB. When the hardware isn't on my desk, I build the tools to test without it.

Before areeba I spent almost seven years at BMB Group, building a B2B merchandising app from scratch with offline sync and receipt printing. I started in 2014 at Code Fish, building HR systems in C# and .NET for clients such as GlobeMed.

As a freelancer I take that discipline to mobile apps, Windows apps and websites for businesses: clear requirements, small working releases, and code that's tested before it ships.

areeba · Android · 2026

Vending machines that take cards

KotlinMDBNexgo UN20 / N62

Turned our payment terminal into the card reader inside vending machines. The machine asks for a price over the MDB bus; the terminal takes the card and tells the machine whether to release the item.

  • Upgraded the MDB library and moved prices to 32-bit, so high-value items charge the right amount.
  • Automatic refund when a paid item doesn't drop, or the machine loses power mid-sale.
  • Self-healing connection and a clear vending screen, tuned for both a phone-size and a square 480×480 terminal.

Personal · Windows · 2026

Vending MDB Simulator

C#WPFRS232

A Windows app that pretends to be a vending machine, so a payment terminal can be tested without one. Choose a product, pay on the real terminal, and watch the machine release it — or fail on purpose.

  • Scenarios for every outcome: sold out, fridge door open, motor jam, power cut, cancel, decline, timeout.
  • A live protocol log that translates every byte on the bus into plain words.
  • Built with tools already in Windows, so anyone on the team can run it.

Questions clients ask

How much does an Android app cost in Lebanon?

It depends on the screens, the backend and the devices it must support. After a short call I send a written scope with milestones and a fixed price or a day rate, your choice, so you know the cost before any work starts.

How long does it take to build an Android app?

A focused first version usually takes 4 to 10 weeks, and AI-assisted development keeps it at the short end. You get a working build every week or two, so you can test it on your phone and steer early.

Can you publish the app on Google Play for me?

Yes. I prepare the store listing, screenshots, privacy policy and release build, and publish it under your own Google Play developer account so you keep full ownership.

Do you also build iPhone apps?

Yes, with Kotlin Multiplatform: one shared codebase for Android and iOS, like the Fly Beirut Taxi app.

Can you take over an app another developer started?

Yes. I start with a short review of the code, tell you honestly what state it's in, then fix the urgent problems first.

Questions clients ask

How much does a website cost in Lebanon?

A simple company site or landing page is a small fixed price; a web app with logins and data is scoped like any software project. After a short call you get a written quote before any work starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page takes a few days, a company website usually one to three weeks, depending on how quickly the content and photos are ready.

Will my website show up on Google?

I set up everything Google needs: titles, descriptions, a sitemap, structured data and Search Console. Ranking takes weeks to months, and a Google Business Profile with reviews helps most for local searches.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and you get the full source code and access to the hosting.

Can you update my site after it's live?

Yes, as small paid updates whenever you need them, or I can set it up so you can change text and photos yourself.
